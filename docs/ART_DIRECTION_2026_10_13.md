## Model and evidence boundary

The male and architecture are editable, authored Three.js geometry. The figure is not a scan, licensed human likeness, motion-capture clip or paid generated GLB. The movement solves walking contact; it is not a collision-enabled game simulation. No Tripo/Meshy credits were spent. The available paid-generation skills require explicit provider invocation and spend approval; none was requested or assumed. The native game-dev CLI and Blender are unavailable here.

The live rendered composition must be checked using the PR’s Chromium/WebGL evidence, separately from the generated architectural poster. Hardware FPS is not inferred from a screenshot or software renderer.

## Reduced motion and media

The reduced layout exposes written requirements and deliverables immediately, makes the portfolio files a normal-flow collection, stops marquees and shows the contact form directly. Semantic labels remain for accessibility even where visible presentation titles are removed.

The optional arrival film adapter still supports `arrival.mp4`, `arrival.webm` and `arrival-with-consultant.mp4`. Architecture-only footage receives the 3D consultant overlay using the same camera contract; a full consultant-inclusive film replaces the scene. Film production is optional for this revision—the default arrival is implemented in Three.js.
